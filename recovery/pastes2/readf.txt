## Playing with other people

Press `K` (or **Play online** in the toolbar) and type a server's address. Every
player signs up with a username and a password the first time and signs in
after that; the server keeps the account, the session and everything the
player does there. Playing alone and playing on a server are kept apart: what
you explore, your trail and your waypoints on a server live on the server, and
none of it touches your own offline save.

On a server you get:

- **Everybody else, in the world and on every map.** Each player gets a colour
  of their own, used for their jacket and wings, their arrow on the minimap and
  the world map, their mark on the compass and on the **locator bar** under it
  (bigger the closer they are, with a notch above or below when they are higher
  or lower), and their name tag.
- **What they have discovered.** In the players list (`Tab`) tick *trail* or
  *discovered* next to anyone — or *everyone* — to draw their trail and the
  ground they have uncovered on your maps, in their colour.
- **Teleport requests, Minecraft-style.** `/tpa name` asks to go to someone,
  `/tpahere name` asks them to come to you; they answer with `/tpaccept` or
  `/tpdeny`, or with `Y` / `N` on the prompt that appears. A request lasts
  forty seconds. Or press `J` for the **teleport menu**: pick *TPA* or *TPA
  here* (`Tab` switches), type a few letters of the name — the list narrows as
  you type — and press `Enter`. Admins also get the instant `/tp`.
- **Chat**: `T` to talk, `/` for commands, `/msg` to whisper, `/near` for the
  players within a few kilometres of you, `/help` for the rest.
- **Proximity voice**, if you switch it on in *Settings → Online*: players near
  you hear you from where you are, fading out with distance — push to talk on
  `Z`, or always on.
- **Swords.** Slot 6 is a sword. Click to swing, Minecraft's way: a full swing
  every 0.625 s does full damage, a falling swing is a critical, and ten
  hearts is a life. The server decides every hit, so nobody can reach
  further or hit harder than the rules allow. Nobody can be hurt within a short
  walk of the spawn, and a fight blocks teleporting away from it for ten
  seconds.
- **No cheats.** On a server the cheat panel, freecam, size changes and pause
  are off, speed is checked, and random teleports are chosen by the server.

To **host a server** — on your own computer for friends on the same wifi, or
on the internet for anyone — see **[HOSTING.md](HOSTING.md)**. It is one Node
process with nothing to install: `node server/server.mjs`, then open
<http://localhost:8080>.

# Hosting a TerraGlide server

TerraGlide plays alone in your browser with nothing but the page. To fly with
other people you need a **server**: one small program that everybody's game
connects to. This guide takes you from "I have never hosted anything" to a
server on the internet with its own address, step by step, and documents
every setting, command and rule along the way.

If you only read one part, read [Quick start](#quick-start-on-your-own-computer)
and then [Over the internet with a tunnel](#over-the-internet-with-a-tunnel-easiest):
together they get friends anywhere in the world flying with you in about ten
minutes, for free, without touching your router.

## Commands and rules

Commands are typed into chat (`T`, or `/` to start with a slash). `/help` lists
the ones you can use. Player names forgive case and can be shortened while the
start is unambiguous (`/tpa ale` for Alexandra).

### Everyone

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/help` | List the commands. |
| `/tpa <player>` | Ask to teleport to a player. |
| `/tpahere <player>` | Ask a player to teleport to you. |
| `/tpaccept [player]` | Accept a teleport request — the newest, or the one from that player. |
| `/tpdeny [player]` | Refuse one. |
| `/tpacancel [player]` | Take back a request you sent. |
| `/tp <player>` | For players, the same as `/tpa`. For admins, see below. |
| `/msg <player> <message>` | Whisper to one player. Also `/w`, `/tell`. |
| `/r <message>` | Reply to whoever last whispered with you. |
| `/near <message>` | Say something only to players within 3 km of you on your world. Also `/l`, `/local`. |
| `/me <action>` | Describe what you are doing. |
| `/list` | Who is online, grouped by world. |
| `/spawn` | Go back to this world's spawn. Once every `spawnCooldownS` (15 s). |

### What is stored, and what never leaves

```text
server/data/
  accounts.json         every account: name, colour, admin, password hash,
                        and the hashes of active sessions
  server.json           bans (with the banned address) and spawn points
                        moved with /setspawn
  players/<name>.json   one player: which world they are on, their health,
                        kills, deaths and time played, and for every world
                        they have visited: where they are, what they have
                        explored, their trail, waypoints and lander
```

Every save writes to a temporary file and renames it over the old one, so a
crash or a power cut mid-save leaves the last good copy, never half a file.
(A leftover `*.tmp` is harmless.) If a file ever cannot be read, the server
does not overwrite it: it renames it to `<file>.broken-<time>` and carries on
without it — see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).
